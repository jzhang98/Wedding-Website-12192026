Our Story

A story of chance, faith, and growing together.

Our story began with a Hinge match in December 2022. Rachel made the first move with a simple “hello” on what turned out to be her first—and last—day on the app. George was half-convinced he was being catfished, but answered anyway. Somehow, that tiny hello became the start of everything.

Our first date was pizza-making and a walk through the park. The next day, Rachel tested positive for Covid and assumed the spark might fade before it had really begun. Instead, George spent the week dropping off medicine, checking in, and planning movie nights from afar. Somewhere in those early days, we also learned that Rachel’s mom remembered George from visiting their salon years earlier in high school—a small reminder that our paths may have crossed long before we knew it.

We quickly discovered how much we shared: the same values, the same hopes for a life built with intention, and personalities different enough to keep life interesting. We made things official on February 3, 2023. Rachel fell in love with George’s honesty, emotional intelligence, and openness; George loved Rachel’s warmth, thoughtfulness, and the joy she brings wherever she goes.

Six months later, George moved to Austin for his MBA, beginning two years of long distance. It was not always easy, but monthly visits, everyday calls, ordinary chores done together over the phone, and constant encouragement made the miles feel smaller. Those years taught us how to choose each other while still giving each other room to grow.

When George returned to Dallas in the summer of 2025, being in the same city again felt like coming home. Then Rachel made the first move one more time and asked, “Would you ever consider a courthouse wedding?” George’s answer was simple: “That’s a great idea.” On October 31, 2025—exactly 1,001 days after becoming official—we were married before a judge and three friends. Now we get to celebrate this next chapter with all of you.

Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

What time should I arrive?

Please arrive by 3:00 PM. Seating opens at that time and the ceremony will begin promptly. To avoid disruptions, guests cannot be seated once the ceremony begins.

What’s the dress code?

Formal / black tie optional. Ladies are encouraged to wear floor-length gowns, cocktail dresses, or dressy separates. Gentlemen may wear suits with a tie. Please avoid casual attire and white, ivory, cream, or colors that could be mistaken for white. White dress shirts are okay.

Is the wedding indoors or outdoors?

The ceremony is planned outdoors, with the reception indoors. In the event of rain or freezing weather, the ceremony will move indoors and guests will be notified by text. Because temperatures may drop after sunset, we recommend bringing a coat, wrap, or shawl.

Will parking be provided?

Yes. Parking is available for all guests on site at The Olana.

Will there be an after-party?

There is not a formally planned after-party. The reception will conclude at 9:00 PM.

How do I RSVP?

Use the RSVP form above. Please submit your response by November 19, 2026.

When should I RSVP by?

Please RSVP by November 19, 2026 so we can finalize seating and catering.

What kind of food will be served?

Guests will enjoy hors d’oeuvres during cocktail hour, followed by dinner prepared by The Olana’s culinary team, wedding cake, and dessert.

Will there be vegetarian, vegan, or gluten-free options?

Yes. Please note dietary restrictions when you RSVP so our caterers can accommodate.

Will there be an open bar?

Yes. We’ll have cocktails, wine, beer, and non-alcoholic options throughout the evening.

Can I take photos during the ceremony?

Yes. You are welcome to take photos, but please keep it to a minimum so everyone can be present with us.

Is there a wedding registry?

Your presence is the greatest gift. If you feel inclined to give, we would deeply appreciate a cash gift toward our honeymoon and future together. A card box will be available at the venue, and Venmo is also available at @RachelJiang99. We will not have a traditional registry.

Can I bring a plus one?

Your invitation is addressed specifically to the names listed. If you were extended a plus one, their name will appear alongside yours when you RSVP.

Are kids welcome?

We truly love and welcome your little ones. As the evening progresses, the celebration will transition into a more formal atmosphere, so please plan according to your family’s comfort.
